About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Waterloo, advised by Prof. Wenhu Chen in TIGER-Lab, and I am affiliated with the Vector Institute. I am also a Student Researcher at Google. Before Waterloo, I was an Applied Scientist at Tencent Smart Healthcare (2021–2023), and I have been working with TIGER-Lab since 2023. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from UC Davis and my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University. In 2025–2026 I was a Research Intern at MiniMax, working on mid-training for agentic LLMs and contributing to MiniMax-M2.

My research asks how to make LLMs both more capable and more measurable. I work on post-training for reasoning (Critique Fine-Tuning teaches models to critique rather than imitate, and works even with a single training problem), evaluation (MMLU-Pro has become a standard benchmark for frontier models; ClawBench tests agents on live websites), and foundation models for coding agents (function-aware fill-in-the-middle mid-training).
