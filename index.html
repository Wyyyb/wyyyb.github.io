Yubo Wang (王钰博)

University of Waterloo; Vector Institute;

I’m a CS Ph.D. student at TIGER-Lab in University of Waterloo, advised by Prof. Wenhu Chen. Previously, I obtained my Master’s degree in Computer Science from UC Davis. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University. From 2021 to 2023, I worked as a full-time NLP Algorithm Engineer at Tencent.

My research focuses on General Artificial Intelligence and its applications. I am particularly interested in understanding the behavior of LLMs, multimodal reasoning, and their evaluation.
